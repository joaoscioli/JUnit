# Interview Defense Notes

Use these notes when a reviewer challenges the project scope or design choices.

## Likely Challenge

"Are these tests meaningful or just written for coverage?"

## Defense

The repository positions tests as executable explanations of behavior. Strong
tests should clarify business rules, expose edge cases, and make refactoring
safer instead of only increasing a coverage percentage.

## Evidence To Show

- `docs/test-style-guide.md`
- `docs/technical-risks.md`
- `docs/acceptance-criteria.md`

## Senior Signal

The decision treats test quality as a design feedback mechanism, not a vanity
metric.
